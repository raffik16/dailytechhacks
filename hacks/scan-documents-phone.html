Scan Documents With Your Phone to Create Perfect PDFs

📅 January 2025⏱️ 6 min read📱 iPhone & Android

You don't need a scanner anymore. Your phone camera combined with the right app creates professional-looking PDFs of documents, receipts, business cards, and more. The results are often better than traditional scanners, and you can do it anywhere.

Built-in Scanning (No App Required)

iPhone: Notes App

Apple's Notes app has a fantastic built-in scanner:

  1. Open Notes → Create new note
  2. Tap the camera icon → Scan Documents
  3. Position document in frame—it auto-captures
  4. Adjust corners if needed → Save
  5. Tap the scan → Share → Create PDF

Features: Auto edge detection, multiple pages, filters (color, grayscale, B&W).

iPhone: Files App

Files app also scans directly:

  1. Open Files → Navigate to folder
  2. Tap ••• → Scan Documents
  3. Saves directly as PDF to that folder

Android: Google Drive

Google Drive has a built-in scanner:

  1. Open Google Drive
  2. Tap + → Scan
  3. Capture document
  4. Crop/adjust → Checkmark to save
  5. Saves as PDF to your Drive

Samsung: Camera App

Samsung phones detect documents automatically:

  1. Point camera at document
  2. Yellow border appears—tap "Scan"
  3. Auto-crops and saves to Gallery

Best Document Scanner Apps

  1. Adobe Scan (Free)

    Best free option with excellent OCR (text recognition):

    • Auto-detects document edges
    • OCR makes text searchable and copyable
    • Saves to Adobe cloud and exports PDF
    • Business card scanning with contact extraction

    Available: iOS, Android

  2. Microsoft Lens (Free)

    Great integration with Microsoft 365:

    • Document, whiteboard, and business card modes
    • Export to Word, PowerPoint, PDF, or OneDrive
    • Excellent whiteboard enhancement
    • OCR included

    Available: iOS, Android

  3. CamScanner

    Popular with powerful features:

    • Smart cropping and enhancement
    • OCR and PDF editing
    • Collaboration features
    • Free tier with ads; premium for more features

    Available: iOS, Android

  4. Genius Scan

    Fast and reliable with great batch scanning:

    • Quick single-tap scanning
    • Batch scanning for multi-page documents
    • Smart page detection
    • Export to many services

    Available: iOS, Android

Tips for Perfect Scans

  1. Use Good Lighting

    Natural light or bright room light works best. Avoid shadows falling across the document. Don't use flash—it creates glare and uneven lighting.

  2. Contrast Your Background

    Place white documents on dark surfaces (or vice versa). This helps apps detect edges automatically.

  3. Keep Document Flat

    Smooth out wrinkles and folds. For books, press pages flat or use book scanning mode in apps.

  4. Shoot Straight Down

    Position your phone directly above and parallel to the document. Most apps correct for angle, but starting straight gives best results.

  5. Fill the Frame

    Get close enough that the document fills most of the screen. More pixels = sharper text.

Scanning Different Document Types

Receipts

  • Scan immediately—thermal receipts fade over time
  • Use high contrast/black & white mode
  • Apps like Expensify scan and extract amounts automatically

Business Cards

  • Adobe Scan and Microsoft Lens extract contact info
  • Save directly to Contacts
  • CamScanner has dedicated business card mode

Whiteboards

  • Microsoft Lens has excellent whiteboard enhancement
  • Removes glare and enhances contrast
  • Straightens perspective automatically

Multi-Page Documents

  • Use batch mode—scan all pages, then save as single PDF
  • Reorder pages before saving
  • Most apps let you add pages to existing scans later

💡 Enable OCR

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) makes scanned text searchable and copyable. Adobe Scan and Microsoft Lens do this automatically. Essential for documents you'll need to search later.

Organizing Scanned Documents

  • Name files descriptively: "2025-01-15_Tax_Receipt_Office_Supplies.pdf"
  • Use cloud storage: Sync to Google Drive, iCloud, or Dropbox
  • Create folders: Receipts, Medical, Tax Documents, Warranties
  • Use tags: Some apps support tags for easy filtering

Conclusion

Your phone is now your scanner. Built-in options (Notes on iPhone, Drive on Android) work great for quick scans. For frequent scanning, Adobe Scan or Microsoft Lens add OCR and better organization. Good lighting and flat documents are the secrets to professional results.
